## Short description (80 characters max)

Tap to replicate, mutate your genome, and evolve a virus across 8 families.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

Start with a single virion under the microscope. Tap to make it copy itself, build replication machinery, and watch your strain mutate before your eyes.

GROW
• Tap the virus to replicate. Tap fast to build a frenzy multiplier up to ×3.
• Build nine kinds of replication machinery, from Ribosome Hijack to Biosphere Bloom.
• Every 25 machines of one kind doubles its output.

MUTATE
• Copy errors become mutations. Each one changes a gene: spike length, spike density, spike tips, pigment, membrane, capsid shape, size and surface proteins.
• Your virus is drawn live in 3D and changes shape as it mutates. No two strains end up looking the same.
• Spend mutations on nine traits across Transmission, Resilience and Virulence.

SURVIVE
• Macrophages drift in and slow your growth. Tap them to fight back and harvest mutations.
• Catch golden recombination events for bursts of virions, extra mutations or a 7× surge.

EVOLVE
• Speciate to jump to a new viral family and keep genome points that boost every future strain.
• Eight families to unlock: Bacteriophage, Picornavirus, Coronavirus, Filovirus, Poxvirus, Orthomyxovirus, Giant Mimivirus and Xenovirus.
• 18 research goals to complete.

IDLE-FRIENDLY
• Your strain keeps replicating while you're away, for up to 8 hours.
• Plays fully offline. No ads, no account, no data collected.

Viral Load is a science-themed idle game. Viruses in the game are stylized and fictionalized for play; nothing in it is medical or scientific advice.
