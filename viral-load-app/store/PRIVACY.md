# Viral Load: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Viral Load is a single-player game published by Southern Edge Electrical.

**Data we collect:** none. The game does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information or usage data.

**Data stored on your device:** your game progress (for example virion counts, upgrades and settings) is saved only in the app's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. You can also erase it in the game with Goals → "Sterilize lab (erase save)".

**Permissions:** the app requests no special permissions.

**Advertising and analytics:** the app contains no ads and no analytics or tracking software.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email listed on the app's Google Play page.
