Field guide

Tap frenzy: tap fast to build a multiplier up to ×3. It fades when you stop.

Induce mutation spends virions to force a mutation right now. Each dose costs more within the same strain.

Macrophages drift in and halve replication. Tap one 5 times to neutralize it and harvest a mutation.

Recombination events glow gold. Tap one for a burst of virions, mutations, or a 7× replication surge.

While the tab is closed your strain goes latent and keeps replicating at a reduced rate for up to 8 hours.
